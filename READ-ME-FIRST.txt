WHAT THIS BATCH DOES

  PALETTE INVERTED.  Dark bands went from nine, to four, to THREE: hero,
  assistant, closingCta. The light rotation was an even third each of cream,
  paper and wash; it is now paper, cream, paper, wash, so white is in the
  majority. The brief wants the site to "read as predominantly white at first
  glance" and navy used "in concentrated places where it adds hierarchy".
  Proof came OUT of the dark set: it was moved to dark a few passes ago to
  stop the testimonials vanishing into a navy page, and with a white page
  around it a warm card with a gold hairline stands out on its own.

  STICKY HEADER, TWO STATES.  Transparent and full height at the top, so the
  hero shows through. Translucent white with a blur, a hairline and less
  padding once scrolled past 24px.

  Every colour in the bar flips with the state. The first version hardcoded
  text-cream at seven call sites and the scrolled state rendered cream on
  white, which is invisible rather than merely wrong. There is one derived
  pair of tokens now, not seven literals.

  AREAS DELETED.  Route, content, both nav entries, the 404 link, the map and
  the town panel. The band type is retired in Bands.tsx and returns null, so a
  content file still carrying one renders nothing rather than crashing.

  The TIGER/Line geometry SURVIVES. BandSeam and BandTexture both draw from
  it, so the site keeps the local drawing without keeping the claim about
  which seven towns Alex will work in.

  SEVEN TOWNS GONE from content: the hero eyebrow, the Lark chip, the buyer
  guide bullet.

  HERO REWRITTEN.  "Relationships first. Real answers from a real guy." is
  replaced with "Real estate decisions should come with clear information."

      home       11 -> 4       buy        10 -> 5
