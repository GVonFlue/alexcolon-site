## What you need up front, in dollars

Buying a house asks for money at four different points before you ever get
the keys, and they are not the same kind of money.

- **Earnest money.** Paid when your offer is accepted, held by a third

- **The inspection.** Paid to the inspector directly, usually the same day
  the inspection happens. [ALEX: your going rate or range.]
- **The appraisal.** Paid to the lender to order it, usually rolled into
  the loan estimate. [ALEX: current range.]
- **Closing costs.** The lender's fees, title work, recording, and
  prepaid items like insurance and property tax reserves. [ALEX: the
  rule of thumb you give people, and whether it changes by loan type.]

Some of this comes back. Earnest money applies toward the purchase; it is
only lost if the buyer backs out for a reason the contract does not
protect. The rest is gone once it is paid, which is exactly why it is
worth knowing in advance rather than the week of closing.

## The order things happen in

From pre-approval to keys, roughly in this order:

1. **Pre-approval.** A lender looks at your income, debt, and credit, and
   tells you what you can borrow. This happens before you look at houses
   seriously, not after you find one.
2. **The search.** Alex sends houses that match what you told him, and
   flags the ones that will not work before you spend a Saturday on them.
3. **The offer.** Price, terms, and how long the seller has to answer.
4. **Inspection.** A licensed inspector walks the house and writes up what
   he finds. This is the point where the deal can still change shape.
5. **Negotiation on the inspection.** Repairs, a price adjustment, or
   neither, depending on what turned up and what you both agree to.
6. **Appraisal.** The lender's own check that the house is worth what you
   are paying for it.
7. **Final loan approval.** The lender clears every remaining condition.
8. **Closing.** Paperwork, funds transfer, and keys, usually in that
   order and usually in one sitting.

## Questions to ask any agent before you sign

Worth asking before you agree to work with anyone, including Alex:

- How many buyers are you working with right now, and what does that mean
  for how fast you can get back to me?
- Do you show houses yourself, or does someone else on a team do the
  showings?
- What happens if I want to end the agreement before we find a house?
- How do you get paid, and does that change based on which house I buy?
- What do you actually do between when we sign and when I close?
